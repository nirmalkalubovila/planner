# Legacy Life Builder: social and conversation scripts

Prepared 1 October 2026. Audience: Sri Lankan students and young professionals. Campaign: **One Goal. One Week.** These are drafts for you to review and publish; nothing has been sent.

## Offer and language

Use a friendly Sinhala-English voice. Keep familiar product words such as goal, plan, week and feedback in English. Read the Sinhala aloud before recording and adjust it to your everyday speech.

The invitation is to try one real goal for seven days during the free public beta. Seven days describes the experiment; it does not mean access expires or billing starts. Do not promise free access forever, guaranteed results, or that the app beats every competitor.

Primary link: https://www.legacylifebuilder.xyz/

Founder story and framework: https://my-site-tawny-alpha.vercel.app/legacy-life

Product explanation: https://my-site-tawny-alpha.vercel.app/tools/legacy-life-builder

Use one destination per post. Send people ready to try the tool directly to the app; use the framework page for posts about your philosophy. Add a direct app invitation to that page before using it as a signup path.

## 14. Production checklist

- Record three original videos per week; adapt captions and endings across platforms.
- Use the current black/gold brand, clear Sinhala-compatible captions and high contrast. Test captions on an ordinary phone.
- Crop into the relevant app action; a whole desktop screen becomes unreadable in a vertical video.
- Demonstrate with fictional goals and demo data. Do not expose real user schedules or feedback without permission.
- Use one action per post: try, reply, or watch. Avoid competing links and demands.
- Add channel tags, for example `?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=one_goal_one_week&utm_content=week1_demo`.
- Track actual signup and activation. Comments and views alone do not show that the app helped.
- For testimonial posts, replace the script with the person's actual approved story. If none exists yet, publish a founder demo or a genuine product improvement instead.
